## Conference talks

**Training on the EMI Model with openCARP and muCARP**  
[MICROCARD Summer Workshop 2026](https://sites.google.com/simula.no/microcard-summer-workshop), in conjunction with the Simula Summer School in Computational Physiology, Oslo, Norway, June 23-24, 2026  
I led practical training and exercises on using openCARP and muCARP for EMI-model simulations of cardiac electrophysiology.

**A Distributed-Memory Framework for the EMI Model**  
MICROCARD Workshop, Strasbourg, France, June 10, 2026  
This talk presents the openCARP EMI implementation: mesh decoupling, membrane-interface reconstruction, volume-surface transfer operators, face-centered ionic evaluation, PETSc/Ginkgo solver backends, and scaling experiments.

**BDDC with Algebraic Adaptivity and Compressed Communication for Cardiac Electrophysiology**  
SIAM Conference on Parallel Processing for Scientific Computing (PP26), Berlin, Germany, March 6, 2026  
This talk connects SDC time integration, algebraic DOF subset selection, BDDC preconditioning, quantized interface exchange, graph-lifting transforms, and compressed communication for large EMI simulations.
